### full car (all sides + rear, no windshield)
The front side windows have 20% window tint. The rear side windows have 20% window tint. The rear windshield has 20% window tint.

### two fronts + full windshield
The front side windows have 20% window tint. The rear side windows have The standard OEM factory glass. The rear windshield has The standard OEM factory glass. CRUCIAL WINDSHIELD SPECIFICATION: The entire front windshield is fully covered in a distinct, dark 35% window tint. The glass must be rendered as dark smoked glass, significantly obscuring the view into the vehicle cabin.

### two fronts + strip
The front side windows have 20% window tint. The rear side windows have The standard OEM factory glass. The rear windshield has The standard OEM factory glass. CRUCIAL WINDSHIELD SPECIFICATION: The main front windshield glass is clear and transparent, but features an extra-dark 5% solid black eyebrow strip running across the top 8 inches of the glass. The strip is precision plotter-cut so its bottom edge forms a perfectly straight, level horizontal line across the full width of the windshield, parallel to the hood line. It does NOT follow the curved factory dot-matrix/frit edge and does not dip or slope down at the corners; the strip is an even height from side to side.
